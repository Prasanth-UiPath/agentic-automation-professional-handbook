🎉 You Built a Coded Agent

You built a Python coded agent and shipped it to the cloud under enterprise governance. Here's your completion checklist.

Completion checklist

Scaffolded the project (uipath new)
Wrote Input/Output models + main()
Mapped the entrypoint in uipath.json
Generated the schema & graph (uipath init)
Tested locally across scenarios (uipath run)
Authenticated to the tenant (uipath auth)
Packaged to a .nupkg (uipath pack)
Published to Orchestrator (uipath publish)
Invoked a cloud job (uipath invoke)
Confirmed the job & output in Orchestrator
