Lesson 11 of 30

Maestro (BPMN)

Orchestrate the Insurance Claim Approval Process on a visual BPMN canvas — coordinating the intake agent you already built, a fraud check, a human supervisor, and GenAI-drafted emails into one governed, end-to-end flow.

Overview

In Module 1, you built the Claim Request Agent — the conversational front door that collects a claim from a claimant, validates the policy, and saves the claim record to Data Fabric. That gets a claim into the system, but it doesn't decide what happens next: who checks it for fraud, who reviews and signs off on it, what happens if it's rejected, and how the claimant is kept informed while all of that is going on.

That's the job of Maestro. In this module, you'll design the Insurance Claim Approval Process as a BPMN diagram — a visual, auditable flow that triggers automatically the moment a new claim record is created in Data Fabric, runs a fraud check, and routes the claim to a human supervisor for validation, branching into an approval or rejection path, each with its own GenAI-drafted email sent back to the claimant.
