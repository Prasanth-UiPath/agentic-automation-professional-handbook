Module 3 · Lesson 3 of 3

Publish & Run

Pack, publish to Orchestrator, invoke, and verify that your agent shows up as an Agent — the four steps that take your tested agent to the cloud.

17

Pack

PowerShell
uipath pack

Look for Project successfully packaged.

18

Publish

PowerShell
uipath publish

Pick your feed when prompted.

19

Invoke

PowerShell
uipath invoke
20

Verify in Orchestrator

Open Orchestrator → Processes and find Settlement_CodedAgent_<your_name>. Its Type should no longer say Function (python).

ℹ️ Governance for free
Because it is now an Orchestrator package, the agent inherits RBAC, audit logs, job monitoring, versioning, and one-click rollback — and it can be invoked by any trigger or UiPath process.
Packaged the agent (uipath pack)
Published to Orchestrator (uipath publish) and picked my feed
Invoked the agent (uipath invoke)
Confirmed in Orchestrator → Processes that the Type is not "Function (python)"

Troubleshooting

SymptomFix

Access denied creating folders or the venvYou are in a system folder or an Administrator window. Redo step 1.
Pasting a multi-line block hangs at the >> promptPress Ctrl+C and use Notepad as in steps 10 to 12.
FutureWarning about uipath.platform.common.constantsHarmless deprecation notice, not an error.
